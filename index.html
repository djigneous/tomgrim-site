TOMGRIM(1)    Lehigh Valley Manual    TOMGRIM(1)


NAME
tomGRIM — dark, warm machine music after midnight


SYNOPSIS
tomgrim [--downtempo] [--breakbeat] [--electro] [--experimental]


DESCRIPTION


tomGRIM is a producer from the Lehigh Valley, PA, making dark and warm downtempo, breakbeat and electro with an experimental streak — slow-burning grooves, broken drums and analog-leaning textures.


The project grew out of the old dj ign3ous (igneous) era; those early tracks are collected here under ELEMENTS. The 2023 album Elements II carried that lineage forward, alongside the singles Radio Flows, No Good and Passing High.
